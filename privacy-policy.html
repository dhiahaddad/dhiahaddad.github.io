Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Dhia Haddad ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile and web applications.

This policy applies to the following apps and related web pages:

  • Blood Donation - a blood donation coordination app.
  • Blood Donation Admin - an administrative app for managing the blood donation platform.
  • Roof Finder - a listing and contact app for roof, housing, or accommodation offers.
Some data categories apply only to specific apps. For example, blood type information applies to Blood Donation, while listing photos and housing preferences apply to Roof Finder.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Profile Information

Depending on the app and features you use, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and authentication identifiers.
  • Profile picture or other optional profile details.
  • Account role, permissions, and administrative access status for Blood Donation Admin.
  • Public profile details that you choose to show to other users.

2.2 Blood Donation Information

For Blood Donation and Blood Donation Admin, we may collect information needed to organize donation activity, such as:

  • Blood type and donation-related preferences.
  • Donation requests, availability, and related status updates.
  • Messages, contact actions, or coordination information between users.
  • Administrative actions used to manage, moderate, or support the platform.

2.3 Roof Finder Listing Information

For Roof Finder, we may collect information related to listings and user interactions, such as:

  • Listing details, location, price, description, amenities, and availability.
  • Photos or media you upload for a listing or profile.
  • Contact information you choose to provide, such as phone, WhatsApp, Facebook, or email.
  • Favorites, hidden fields, viewing preferences, and other app settings.

2.4 Location Information

With your permission or when you provide a location manually, we may use location information to:

  • Find nearby blood donation centers, donors, recipients, or urgent requests.
  • Display or search Roof Finder listings by location.
  • Open map links or help users understand listing location information.
  • Provide location-based notifications or relevant search results.

2.5 Device, Usage, and Technical Information

We may automatically collect technical information, including:

  • Device type, operating system, app version, and language settings.
  • Push notification tokens and device identifiers.
  • App usage events, diagnostics, crash reports, and security logs.
  • Data-saver or display preferences that help control app behavior.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide blood donation, administration, listing, messaging, and contact features.
  • Show relevant listings, requests, profiles, notifications, and app content.
  • Upload and display user-selected images and listing photos.
  • Maintain platform safety, prevent abuse, and support moderation.
  • Improve app reliability, user experience, and security.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to valid requests.

4. Permissions Explained

4.1 Location Access

Why we need it: To support nearby donation features, location-based listing search, map links, and location-aware notifications.

When we access it: Only when you use features that need location or grant permission through your device settings.

Your control: You can disable location access in your device settings at any time.

4.2 Photos and Media

Why we need it: To let you choose and upload profile pictures or Roof Finder listing photos.

When we access it: Only when you select media or upload an image.

Your control: You can revoke photo or media permissions in your device settings.

4.3 Notifications

Why we need it: To send important alerts, donation updates, listing updates, administrative notices, or app safety messages.

When we use it: Only for app-related notifications and important service messages.

Your control: You can disable notifications in your device settings or app settings where available.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in limited circumstances:

  • With other users: Information you choose to make visible, such as public profile details, donation request details, listing details, or contact options.
  • With administrators: Information needed to manage, moderate, support, or secure the relevant app.
  • With service providers: Trusted providers that help operate authentication, storage, hosting, notifications, analytics, or crash reporting.
  • For legal compliance: When required by law or to protect rights, safety, and platform integrity.

6. Third-Party Services

Our apps may use third-party services including Firebase, Google Sign-In, Google Maps or map links, Facebook login or profile/contact links, push notification services, analytics, and crash reporting tools. These services may process data according to their own policies.

Firebase privacy information is available at: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including access controls, secure backend services, and limited access to personal information. No system is completely secure, but we work to reduce risk and respond to security issues appropriately.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the apps, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce policies. You may request deletion of your account and associated data using our Data Deletion Request Form.

9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing.
  • Export or receive a copy of your data where technically feasible.

To request account deletion, visit the Data Deletion Request Page.

10. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe we collected information from a child under 16, please contact us.

11. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence. We use service providers that may store or process data internationally and rely on appropriate safeguards where required.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy
